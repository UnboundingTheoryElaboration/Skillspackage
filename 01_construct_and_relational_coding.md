# Skill 01: Construct Discovery and Method-Neutral Relational Coding (`01_construct_and_relational_coding.md`)

## 1. Scope
Operates in **Discovery Mode** (inductive construct and relationship generation) or **Application Mode** (scoring an approved codebook across empirical cases and conceptual records).

## 2. Procedure
1. **Method-Neutral Relational Evidence Extraction**:
   - Before assigning numbers, record relational statements in `relational_evidence_register.csv`: `relationship_id`, `source_id`, `pdf_page`, `evidence_type` (`empirical_case` vs. `conceptual_or_field`), `epistemic_basis`, `actors_and_constructs`, `reported_relationship`, `temporal_phase` (`T0_entry`, `T1_trigger`, `T2_mobilization`, `T3_outcome_regime`), and `alternative_readings`.
2. **Multi-Dimensional Outcome & Construct Separation (`G2A_codebook`)**:
   - Check whether the focal phenomenon is one-dimensional or multi-dimensional (e.g., separating **Substantive Decision Authority [`DEC_AUTH`]** from **Epistemic Generativity & Practice Renewal [`EPI_GEN`]**).
   - Enforce strict operational independence between explanatory conditions and outcomes.
3. **Evidence-Linked Scoring & Explicit `NULL` Abstention (`G2B_coding`)**:
   - Score empirical cases on the `1–5` substantive rubric (`1 = Full out`, `2 = Mostly out`, `2.9 = Crossover`, `3 = More in than out`, `4 = Mostly in`, `5 = Full in`) with verbatim page-located quotes (`8–45 words`).
   - Record `NULL` (`None`) whenever a study is silent on a construct.
