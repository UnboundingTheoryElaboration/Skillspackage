# Skill 07: Theory Elaboration Register, Reverse Lineage, and Adversarial Critique (`07_theory_elaboration_and_critique.md`)

## 1. Structured Theory Elaboration Register (`theory_elaboration_register.csv`)
Rather than merely naming Boolean terms, every theoretical contribution must be structured as an explicit elaboration of prior theory:
- `proposition_id` & `elaboration_type` (`clarification`, `boundary_specification`, `mechanism_refinement`, `relationship_revision`, `integration`, or `simplification`)
- `existing_theoretical_claim` & `limitation_or_anomaly`
- `proposed_elaboration`
- `multi_method_signature` (Set-analytic recipe + Process sequence `T0..T3` + Regime `eta^2` profile)
- `supporting_and_deviant_cases` (with verbatim PDF quotes and page numbers)
- `conceptual_and_field_corroboration` (linking synthesis-only records with their epistemic-basis labels)
- `rival_elaboration` & `method_dependence` (whether the claim survives across QCA, Process, and Regime pathways)
- `disconfirming_evidence_needed`

## 2. Claim-Specific Limitations & Ranked Next Empirical Designs
Every report concludes with a Claim-Specific Limitations Matrix and at least four ranked next-generation empirical strategies evaluated by inferential gain, data requirements, and feasibility.
